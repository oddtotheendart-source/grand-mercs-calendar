# Public-site source restrictions

## Mandatory privacy rule: MERCS
All READNEWS MERCS articles, from the first article through the end of every supplied span, are private internal office notes. Never publish them on the website or in this public repository.

This prohibition covers original text, excerpts, screenshots, downloadable files, donor/personnel details, summaries, paraphrases, and facts derived solely from MERCS. Do not publish MERCS material even if it seems relevant to history or event planning.

MERCS is distinct from MERCHANTS and TRADERS. Do not merge these board names or treat them as aliases.

Keep mixed raw logs outside this public repository and all deployed assets. Separate records by their actual news-board header before analysis. Each proposed public addition must have a traceable, independently permitted source. If provenance is missing or the source is MERCS, omit it from public output.

The automated check detects explicit MERCS article references. It cannot detect every unlabeled excerpt or paraphrase; source review is mandatory before every public update.
