Bio

Xaris's Many Trades

One craftsman. Far too many tools.

Need something forged, stitched, mixed, marked, or fitted? Xaris accepts commissions across a broad range of trades:

  • Master Armoursmithing and Weaponsmithing
  • Tailoring for practical and fashionable needs
  • Remedies, Transmutation, and Inkmilling
  • Harvesting, Extraction and Gathering
  • Heraldry and Shipfitting
  • Runelore
  • Tattoos, and Inkmilling
  • Design services
  • General labor

From steel and sailcloth to inks, herbs, and heraldic seals, bring the job to Xaris. If he lacks the proper tool, it may simply be hiding under another one.
