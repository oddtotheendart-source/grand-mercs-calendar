Shared Crucible Structure

1Brief

Rules, ingredients, materials, or another common challenge are provided in advance as appropriate to the trade.

2Create

Entrants prepare their design during the lead-up period.

3Present

The finished work is presented at the live event.

4Judge

Judging focuses on skill, creativity, presentation, and how well the entry answers the challenge.

Ingredient-list brainstorming — not contest requirements: 1 protein, 3 starches, and 4 vegetables were ideas raised while discussing what the list might need. No ingredient counts have been agreed.

Attendance raffle: current idea is to include everyone present, including judges, in a separate random prize drawing.

Venue idea: the Park in Delos has been suggested as an open public setting.

House participation: the goal is to place active Merchants into visible host, judge, support, and trade-specific roles as the series develops.
