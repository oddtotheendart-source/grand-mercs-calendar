In 649 AF Kitarel reported that she had served as leader for 101 years. Her tenure spans the transition from the Merchants of the Crown to the Grand Merchant Collective and the move from Hashan to Delos.

Keeper of the Coin

575 AF
Identity & direction

A House reconsidering its direction

Camande clarified that a referendum about “change” did not itself abandon the House's founding mission as Merchants. It asked whether the House should explore options in response to pressures affecting that identity.

Merchant News #4168
575 AF
Patronage

Lord Phaestus returns as Patron

Kitarel announced the accepted return of Lord Phaestus, the Lord Smith, as House Patron.

Merchant News #4170
578 AF
Head of Newcomers / D.I.E.

Dalerio resigns; Rispok takes newcomer leadership

Dalerio resigned as Head of Newcomers. Rispok was appointed to the role, creating a vacancy for Director of Innovation and Events. Rispok then reported his plans to review newcomer scrolls, projects, and training.

Merchant News #4176, #4179–#4180
584–585 AF
Shopkeeping / Administration

Shop and administrative posts change hands

Wattsee's absence opened the Head House Shopkeeper position. Lavinia later announced her appointment. Adonay then announced semi-retirement, opening the selection of a new House Administrator.

Merchant News #4194, #4197–#4198
592–627 AF
Director of Innovation and Events

The D.I.E. office repeatedly turns over

Darlah resigned in 592 AF after years of events work. Later reports record Tierny's absence creating another vacancy, followed by a later departure by Darlah from the position in 627 AF. The archive preserves the openings as reported rather than filling unrecorded intervals.

Merchant News #4223, #4276, #4298
611–614 AF
Newcomers / Internal Affairs / Library

Education and internal offices are reorganised

Hhaos left the Head of Newcomers and Blade Path posts, opening two vacancies. Soon after, D.I.E. was designated a non-executive director role, a new Internal Affairs post was opened, and the House Librarian position was advertised following Gawi's absence.

Merchant News #4262, #4266–#4267
633 AF
Major transition

From the Merchants of the Crown to the Grand Merchant Collective of Delos

With the Delos bazaar nearing completion, Kitarel announced that the main headquarters would move from Hashan's Citadel of Prosperity to Delos. The House would pursue a broader identity as a banner for Merchants across Sapience. The following report states that the Hashan chapter had come to its completion and records Kitarel under the title Keeper of the Coin.

The new estate included public rooms, a stable and bar, with the House Hall and specialised facilities within the Delos estate.

Merchant News #4315–#4317
637 AF
Council structure

Non-executive director positions dissolved

Kitarel announced that non-executive director posts were removed from House structure. Event and library responsibilities were redistributed across Council rather than assigned to a single non-executive office.

Merchant News #4334
643–644 AF
Council consolidation

Four formal Council roles are defined, then consolidated

The House published duties for Dean of Admittance, Administrative Officer, Headmaster, and Investment Manager. With low application numbers, the next restructuring combined Headmaster with Dean of Admittance under the Headmaster title, and combined financial, administrative, and investment work under Administrative Officer.

Merchant News #4351, #4357
649–650 AF
Leadership change

Kitarel closes a 101-year leadership tenure; Sheltan elected

Kitarel described 101 years of leadership, first as Overseer of Prosperity and then Keeper of the Coin, and expressed hope of passing the mantle. Sheltan contested, won the election 7–5 over Rispok, and was formally recorded as House Leader.

Merchant News #4367, #4370–#4373
669 AF
Leadership change

Laytron enters House leadership

During the leadership contest, Laytron cited previous CrownMerchant leadership, novice and apprentice work, investment management, and the need to restore momentum. The subsequent House election records Laytron as the new House Leader.

Merchant News #4410, #4418–#4419
670–699 AF
Archive note

Education, meetings, appointments, shops and anniversary work continue

The recovered reports in this period include Council openings, meeting minutes, novice-program updates, shopkeeping, design contests, and the House's 50th anniversary. The formerly missing #4454–#4482 block has been located in the supplied log. Office succession in this interval awaits review of the captured text.

Recovered run #4420–#4453 and #4483 onward; #4454–#4482 located, review pending
709–710 AF
House / Council restructure

Advancement, combat, shops, public image and seafaring receive defined roles

Laytron created Advancement Advocate and appointed Nilgeam, while Sir Tyamat was asked to support combat and security learning. After Alarke's departure and Rispok stepping down, Headmaster was abolished and Head of Newcomers took responsibility for novices and apprentices.

A later appointment report names Ashelia as Head Shopkeeper, Korimir for public image and relations, Spas for the House ship and trade work, and Illyose for security and combat.

Merchant News #4498, #4500–#4501
717–723 AF
D.I.E. / Council

Rapid D.I.E. changes and a root-and-branch Council review

Adaele stepped down as D.I.E.; Laniara was appointed, and Jezabelle later appears in the role after Laniara's departure. Korimir's public-image position passed to Zerda. Laytron then opened every Council role for review.

The resulting 723 AF structure appointed Sarya as Dean of Admittance, Zerda as Advancement Advocate, retained Madelyne as Head Shopkeeper, reappointed Jezabelle as D.I.E., retained Gawi and Illyose, and abolished Ambassador to Sovereign Affairs.

Merchant News #4509–#4510, #4515–#4517, #4519–#4521
762–769 AF
Dean / Advancement / D.I.E. / Shopkeeping

Another Council reshuffle

Arralana and Sarya swapped duties: Arralana became Dean of Admittance and Sarya Advancement Advocate. Later, Alizais stepped down from D.I.E.; Auri took the post for an initial two-year period. Madelyne stepped down as Head Shopkeeper and Ozmatiah accepted the position.

Merchant News #4538, #4544–#4546
812–814 AF
D.I.E. / Dean of Admittance

Vadora takes D.I.E.; Tychondrius takes newcomer education

Laytron appointed Vadora Director of Innovation and Events, succeeding Auri. Kresslack later stepped down as Dean of Admission / Head of Novices, and Tychondrius reported taking the Dean of Admittance office.

Merchant News #4553, #4555–#4556
884 AF
Recorded House debate

Hashan relationship discussed in the news

Laytron relayed a request for a Merchant speaker in a Hashani ritual on growth and Progress. Kresslack responded with a different view, stressing the Collective's independence after leaving Hashan for Delos and arguing for impartiality. These are preserved as distinct reported positions.

Merchant News #4584–#4585
933 AF
Library / historical record

A House history book enters the library

Moritza reported that a book detailing the history of the House had been placed in the quiet corner of the library and requested more works concerning history and commerce.

Merchant News #4600
940 AF
Order of the Coin

Charter of Honour submitted

Bumin Rousseau published the Charter of Honour submitted to the Knights Guild for the Order of the Coin, tying the Merchant House to a formal chivalric framework centred on commerce, honour and integrity.

Merchant News #4609–#4610
969–974 AF
Estate development

Suggestion system and amphitheatre added

The estate suggestion box was connected to House logs so anonymous proposals could be seen by the House. Later, an amphitheatre designed through Mariko's work was added near the market stalls for public performances and gatherings.

Merchant News #4612–#4613
987–989 AF
Merchant Manor

The Merchant Manor opens and begins hosting House activity

News reports advertise Mayaween activity at the Manor, followed by Laytron's grand-opening announcement. Private rooms were subsequently offered within the Manor.

Merchant News #4616–#4619
993–1003 AF
Director of Innovation and Events

Mariko steps back; Ticca appears as D.I.E.

Mariko stepped back from active House duty, opening the D.I.E. office. In 998 AF Laytron again described the coming appointment as part of a broader House revitalisation. By 1003 AF, Ticca Indasha signs the World Games design-contest results as Director of Innovation and Events.

Merchant News #4620, #4622–#4623
998–1010 AF
Restructuring / House planning

New support roles and renewed House planning

The 998 AF restructuring opened House Scribe, House Librarian and House Warden, and proposed a team under Kassie to support advancement from HR3 to HR5. The 1010 AF meetings returned attention to the Merchant Manor, Delosian Commons, social events, a special House event, sailing, procurement, dungeoneering, scrolls, library work and capital investment.

Merchant News #4622, #4624–#4626
1014 AF
Patronage

Prospero's patronage returns to discussion

Laytron reported the opportunity for Lord Prospero to return as House Patron, noting that Prospero had served as patron twice before and asking members for ideas to mark the occasion.

Project context & donation totals

Earlier announcement: Kitarel’s “Grand Merchant Auction” (Merchant News #4335, 12 Phaestian 637 AF; 13 October 2013) proposed Gold and Silver Dragon talisman sets among possible auction lots to raise funds for an Eleusis shop. Tyamat was spearheading the event, and PROJECT 413 tracked donations. The announcement says talisman collection was already underway, so it is an early located announcement rather than proof of the project’s first post.

Silver is named as a proposed set. Neither this announcement nor Rispok’s Gold donation report establishes that a Silver set was completed.

Merchant Rispok, Indefatigable Lupine, recorded donations from Artanis, Gamoneterik, Kitarel, Nat, Rispok, and Tyamat. His message states that the Gold Dragon Talisman was not fully completed and was sold piecemeal.

The supplied list records 413 level-1 pieces. These totals count donated pieces; they do not establish a completed talisman or sale proceeds.
