Com Market

Visit the Commodities Market in Delos to buy or sell. Prices in these commands are per commodity.

BrowseCM LIST [comm] [maxprice]
Your listingsCM LIST MY [comm]
BuyCM BUY <num> <comm> FOR <max price>
SellCM SELL <num> <comm> FOR <price per>
Cancel a listingCM CANCEL <id>
